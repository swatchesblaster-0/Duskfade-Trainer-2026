## 🎮 About the Game

Duskfade is a single-player 3D action-platformer developed by Weird Beluga and published by Fireshine Games. It released on August 13, 2026. Players control Zirian and his mechanical companion Cuckoo as they explore clockwork fantasy realms, fight enemies, collect upgrades, and attempt to save Zirian's sister from the Clock Tower.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Duskfade**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and gameplay adjustments for Duskfade on PC. It can simplify platforming, exploration, collectible cleanup, and mod-and-slash encounters while leaving the original story and level structure intact.

**Target Audience:** Players who want easier combat, more forgiving platforming, faster ability progression, or a convenient way to complete Duskfade's collectible-focused exploration.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Invincibility** — Prevents Zirian from losing health during platforming sections, enemy encounters, and boss battles.
- **Unlimited Ability Energy** — Keeps energy available for Duskfade's ranged attacks, defensive actions, and other unlocked combat abilities.
- **Collectible Quantity Editor** — Adjusts supported collectible totals, including upgrade resources used to obtain combat improvements and other rewards.
- **Instant Upgrade Currency** — Provides a convenient way to increase available resources for purchases and character upgrades.
- **Game Speed Control** — Adjusts the overall game speed for more relaxed platforming or faster traversal through cleared areas.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Unlimited Traversal Uses** — Allows repeated use of unlocked movement tools such as the grapple, glide, dash, and rail-grinding abilities.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **One-Hit Defeat** — Causes standard enemies to fall after a successful damaging attack.
- **Freeze Enemies** — Stops affected enemies from acting while leaving Zirian free to reposition and attack.
- **Damage Multiplier** — Increases the damage dealt by Zirian's sword combos and unlocked special attacks.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Duskfade combines third-person platforming with jumping, dashing, grappling, gliding, and rail grinding. Use the movement options normally while learning each area's routes, then activate Unlimited Traversal Uses when practicing difficult sections or revisiting locations for missed collectibles. Invincibility can help prevent interruptions from hazards during extended platforming sequences.

### Tools & Inventory

Progression revolves around collectible resources, upgrade materials, time-themed items, and unlockable abilities rather than a large equipment inventory. Collectibles can be exchanged for upgrades and other rewards. Use the Collectible Quantity Editor only after saving your game, and allow the game to register the modified total before opening a shop or purchasing an upgrade.

### World & Environment

Zirian travels through distinct fantasy environments connected by the larger story of the Clock Tower and the eternal night. Many areas contain paths and collectibles that become accessible only after new movement or combat abilities are unlocked. For cleanup runs, use Game Speed Control and Unlimited Traversal Uses to move efficiently through previously explored locations.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat uses sword-based mod-and-slash attacks, target-focused encounters, dodges, defensive timing, ranged abilities, and additional moves unlocked during progression. Invincibility is useful for learning enemy patterns and boss phases, while Damage Multiplier and One-Hit Defeat shorten routine encounters. Freeze Enemies can provide a controlled environment for testing combos and special attacks.
